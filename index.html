1. Information Collection

Mishkat Al-Hoda ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account or use guest mode, we collect:

  • Email address (only for registered accounts)
  • Name or username for personalized experience
  • Account preferences and settings
  • Progress data (Quran reading progress, prayer tracking)
Guest Mode: You can use the app without registration. In guest mode, your data is stored locally on your device only and is not synced to our servers.

1.2 Types of Data Collected

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Personal Identification Data: Name, email address (for registered accounts only)
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates, city, country (for prayer times and Qibla direction)
  • Usage Data: App interactions, feature usage, session duration, crash reports
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Progress Data: Quran reading progress, prayer tracking, bookmarked verses
  • Preference Data: App settings, theme preferences, notification preferences

1.3 How We Collect Data

Data is collected through:

  • Direct User Input: Information you provide when creating an account or using features
  • Automated Collection: Usage analytics and crash reports through Firebase Analytics
  • Device Sensors: Location data from GPS (with user permission)
  • Third-Party Services: Authentication via Google Sign-In (optional)

1.4 Location Data

The App requests location permissions to provide:

  • Accurate prayer times for your location
  • Qibla direction calculation
  • Nearby mosque locations

Location data is used only when the relevant features are enabled.

1.5 Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage data to:

  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Understand feature usage patterns
  • Personalize your experience based on your interactions

6. Account Deletion and Data Removal

6.1 How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from within the app by following these steps:

  1. Open the Mishkat Al-Hoda app on your device
  2. Navigate to Settings (usually represented by a gear icon)
  3. Select Account Settings or Profile
  4. Tap on "Delete Account" button
  5. Read the warning message carefully
  6. Enter your password for verification (if applicable)
  7. Confirm your decision to permanently delete the account
Important: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered. If you have an active subscription, deleting your account will cancel it immediately.

6.2 What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you delete your account through the app:

  • All personal data is permanently deleted from our servers
  • Your progress data (Quran reading, prayer tracking) is removed
  • Your account preferences and settings are erased
  • Any active subscriptions are automatically cancelled
  • You will not be charged for any future billing cycles
  • No refunds will be issued for the current billing period
  • You will be immediately logged out of the app
  • All app data associated with your account is removed

6.3 Subscription Cancellation Policy

If you have an active subscription:

  • Automatic Cancellation: Deleting your account through the app automatically cancels any active subscription
  • Separate Cancellation: If you only want to cancel subscription without deleting account:
    • For iOS users: Cancel through App Store settings
    • For Android users: Cancel through Google Play Store
    • Or use the in-app subscription management section
  • Refund Policy: No refunds are provided for the current billing period after cancellation
  • Access Duration: You retain access to premium features until the end of the current billing period

6.4 Data Retention After Deletion

After account deletion:

  • All personal data is removed from our active databases within 24 hours
  • Complete deletion from backup systems occurs within 30 days
  • Anonymous, aggregated data may be retained for analytics purposes
  • Legal/regulatory required data may be retained as necessary by law
  • Subscription billing records are retained as required by financial regulations
